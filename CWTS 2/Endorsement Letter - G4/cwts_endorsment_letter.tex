% Body of the letter
We are NSTP-CWTS students from the University of the Philippines 
College of Human Kinetics, Diliman, Quezon City. As part of our 
academic requirements, we are tasked to implement our nation-building 
community engagement project entitled \textbf{``\textit{Rampanalo}: 
Audiovisual Art as a Celebration of Pride and Power for the Home of 
the Golden Gays''}. Said student initiative shall be held on October 
5, 2026, with the aim of fulfilling the following objectives:

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Produce a documentary about the Home of the Golden Gays;
    \item Promote self-expression and creativity through movement, 
		  performance art, and fashion;
    \item Provide a platform for elder queers to share their stories 
		  and perceive solidarity from the queer community;
    \item Strengthen overall support for the queer community and 
		  increase awareness for the struggles and experiences.
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{0.5em}

Crucial to the accomplishment of these objectives, we kindly request
your support in facilitating the production and filming during the 
first two weeks of October at your foundation. The project will involve 
at least twenty local participants, all of whom are residents and 
beneficiaries of your foundation, and whom we envision to benefit 
greatly from this initiative. We have attached our planned itinerary 
details for your perusal.

With this juncture, may we respectfully request on your good end, your 
consent to be interviewed, filmed, and to allow us to document your 
location and beneficiaries, to help us realize our nation-building 
project within the stated time period? Your kind assistance would mean 
a great deal to us, as it will not only help us fulfill our academic 
requirements, but also enable us to achieve our nation-building aspirations 
and, more importantly, deliver the intended benefits of our project to 
the community.

Thank you very much!
