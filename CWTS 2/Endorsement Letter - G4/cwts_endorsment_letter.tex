% Body of the letter
We are NSTP-CWTS students from the University of the Philippines College
of Human Kinetics, Diliman, Quezon City. As part of our academic requirements,
we are tasked to implement our nation-building community engagement project
entitled \textbf{``\textit{Rampanalo}: Audiovisual Art as a Celebration of Pride and Power for
the Home of the Golden Gays''}. Said student initiative shall be held on October
5, 2026, with the aim of fulfilling the following objectives:

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Promote self-expression and creativity through movement, performance art, and fashion
	\item Provide a platform for elder queers to share their stories and perceive solidarity from the queer community
	\item Strengthen overall support for the queer community and increase awareness for the struggles and experiences
\end{enumerate}

To accomplish these objectives, we offer two options for the Golden Gays community. 
The foundation is to deliberate which activity would be more feasible: 1.) to
participate in the creation of a documentary on the foundation, or 2.) to facilitate
a fundraising workshop on the foundation

Crucial to the accomplishment of these objectives, we need your kind and enabling 
help in the conduct of the production and filming or workshop development on the 
first two weeks of October at your foundation. The project will involve at least twenty local voluntary
participants, residents composed of your beneficiaries, whom we envision to benefit
much from this initiative. We have attached our planned itinerary details for your
perusal.

At this point, we kindly ask you to choose only one (1) of the two options above;
either the documentary or the workshop, whichever you believe would be more suitable
and beneficial for your community. We have left the specific details of each option
attached for your input and preference.

With this juncture, may we please request on your good end to be amenable to
interviews, filming, and allow us to document your location and your beneficiaries
(if you choose the documentary), or to facilitate the workshop sessions (if you choose
the workshop), to help us realize our nation-building project within the said time
period. Your kind help would mean so much for us because it will not only help us
fulfill our academic requirements, but will also enable us to realize our
nation-building aspirations, and more importantly deliver the benefit-outcomes of
our project for the community.
